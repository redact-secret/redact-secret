# Code of conduct

Redact Secret adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who participates in the
project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any other
channel the project runs, in every `redact-secret` repository.

## Our standards

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, accept review
of your work gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project's users.
Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. The
Contributor Covenant text linked above is the authoritative statement.

This project also has a rule specific to its subject: never post a real
credential, token, private key, or other live secret in any project space,
even to demonstrate a detector gap. Use unmistakably synthetic or revoked
examples (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). A real secret posted by mistake is
removed as soon as it is seen, and the poster should revoke it.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#roles). Open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/redact-secret/redact-secret/security/advisories/new)
when the concern involves an exposed secret or another security matter;
otherwise contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Do not
open a public issue to report a conduct problem.

All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainers decide
the response following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban). A maintainer who is the
subject of a report takes no part in deciding it.
